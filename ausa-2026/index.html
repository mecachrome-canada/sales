What we machine

Medium to large structural parts, sub-assemblies and complete work packages, built to print and delivered as finished kits when needed.

Defense

  • Structural fittings and frames for military platforms
  • Pylon, mount and interface components in titanium
  • Missile launch rail / support
  • Optronic and sensor housings

Aerostructures

  • Wing ribs, spars, beams and structural fittings
  • Landing gear components and sub-assemblies
  • Trunnions and critical rotorcraft fittings

Space

  • Satellite structural and shear panels
  • L-band antenna structures and instrument support panels
  • Launcher structural fittings and longerons

What is in our scope

In-house

  • Raw-material procurement and stock management
  • 3- to 5-axis machining in aluminum, titanium and stainless steel, from plate, block or forging
  • CAM programming, deburring, dimensional control (CMM & laser), FAI
  • Assembly, touch-up paint and sealant, kitting

Through our supply chain

  • Surface treatments, chemical processing and full paint
  • Non-destructive testing (penetrant, ultrasonic)
  • Forging and casting supply, when the part calls for it

Build-to-print: we manufacture to your design and specifications.
